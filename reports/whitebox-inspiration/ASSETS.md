# Homepage artwork

Created with the built-in Image Generation tool on 4 November 2026 and recovered from its saved output when implementation resumed on 5 November. Both are original generated assets, with no input reference image. The Whitebox images in `reference-assets/` are not used by the application.

Final project assets:

- `public/images/marketing/evalforge-study.webp`: 2172 × 724, 97,522 bytes. Original illustration for the homepage footer. It is served directly as a compressed WebP and loaded lazily.
- `public/images/marketing/evalforge-geometry.webp`: 1536 × 1024, 20,262 bytes. Decorative hero background.

FFmpeg encoded the saved PNGs to WebP at quality 88 and 90 respectively; their composition was not edited.

## Footer prompt

Create one original website footer illustration for EvalForge, a code review practice product. Ultra-wide panoramic composition, aspect ratio 3:1, target 2304 by 768. Art direction: carefully crafted cinematic pixel art with restrained finely resolved pixels, a quiet late-night engineering study in a converted brick loft. Interior seen straight on: broad dark industrial window overlooking an understated rainy city and distant trees, a wooden desk at the RIGHT with a small mint-green-glowing monitor showing only indistinct abstract code-like lines, closed books, a paper notebook and a warm brass desk lamp. NO people, NO anime characters. Rich charcoal, ink blue-green and muted sage/mint, with just a small pool of warm amber desk light. LEFT 45 percent deliberately quiet dark window and wall, with much less detail and light, so real HTML heading and CTA can overlay. Upper area dark. Bottom fade naturally into very dark charcoal #0c0e12. Atmospheric, tactile and editorial, attractive enough to anchor a premium developer product. The desk and city are tangible and human, not a generic tech circuit board. Wide full-bleed scene, no decorative frame, no vignette circle. Original scene and composition, not a recreation of any existing image. No text, no readable words, no logo, no watermark, no UI chrome.

## Geometric backdrop prompt

Create a minimal abstract geometric background asset for a premium code review website hero. Landscape 3:2 aspect, target 1536 by 1024. Flat near-black charcoal background #0c0e12. Crisp extremely fine muted gray-green lines form 12 to 18 very large irregular convex polygon cells, an understated Voronoi mesh like quiet architectural linework. The polygons are elongated and asymmetric, with largest open spaces across the center-left where headline copy will go. A few intersections and short line segments near the far right have a restrained pale mint accent. Very low contrast overall: most of the image is near-black, lines around dark desaturated gray-green. No gradients, no glowing blobs, no stars, no particles, no 3D, no texture, no noise, no text, no symbols, no icons, no diagram labels, no people. The geometry is exact crisp straight linework and empty space. It should read as subtle background structure, not a focal illustration.
